## 1. The 60-second version (say this out loud to a prospect)

> "Every missed call is a customer who just called your competitor instead. I can show you — in the next two minutes — an AI that answers every call, books the appointment, and never sleeps. Want to hear it?"

Then hand them the demo line. Let the product do the selling.

---

## 2. Client pitch script (use this on your own local-business prospects)

**Opening (in person or by phone):**

> "Quick question — when your business is closed, or every line's busy, what happens to that call? [Let them answer.] That's usually a lost job, right? I want to show you something that fixes exactly that."

**The demo (this is the whole pitch):**

> "Call this number right now: [DEMO LINE]. That's an AI agent — it answers instantly, asks the right questions for a business like yours, and books straight into a calendar. It's not a chatbot bolted onto your phone, it's built to actually close the booking."

**The math (make it their number, not a stat):**

> "If you're missing even [2-3] calls a week at an average job of $[X], that's roughly $[calculate]/month walking out the door. The agent costs a fraction of that."

**The close:**

> "Setup is a flat $997, then a monthly fee to keep it running — no long-term contract. Want me to get you booked in?"

**Common objections, answered:**

- *"Will it sound robotic?"* → "That's exactly why I had you call the demo line first — you already heard it. Did it sound robotic to you?"
- *"What if it books something wrong?"* → "It's built with your exact hours, services, and rules — it doesn't guess, and anything it's unsure about gets escalated to a human number you choose."
- *"I already have voicemail / an answering service."* → "Right, and how many of those calls actually turn into a booking? This one does the booking itself, on the first call."

## 3. Where to find clients (the realistic version)

- Vertical-specific Facebook Groups (HVAC contractors, salon owners, med spa owners, dentists) — join as a genuine participant first, don't cold-pitch on day one
- Local chamber of commerce and small-business associations — in-person still works for this audience
- Cold outreach (call/email/in-person) to businesses you can already see are missing calls — call their main line yourself at an odd hour and see what happens
- Your own existing client base, if you already run a marketing/consulting practice

## 4. Where to find *other partners* (if you're building a team)

The "AI automation agency" community is large and active — Liam Ottley's AI Automation Agency Hub and Nate Herk's AI Automation Society (both on Skool, 300K+ members each) are full of people already looking for exactly this kind of productized offer. A specific, numbers-driven post about real results — not a generic pitch — is what gets traction there.
