Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026 · Meridian Agency, Ontario, Canada

Meridian Agency ("Meridian," "we," "us") installs Voice, Sales, and Booking AI agents for local businesses. This page explains what we collect, why, and your rights under Canadian privacy law (PIPEDA) and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: email, business name, phone (proposal form, intake form, checkout)
  • Business operating details you provide for your agent: hours, services, FAQs, booking rules
  • Payment details — processed directly by Stripe; Meridian never stores card numbers
  • Basic usage data: chat messages sent to your agent or our site guide, for support and quality

Why we collect it

  • To generate your proposal and configure your AI agent
  • To deliver your connect guide, API key, and setup instructions
  • To process payment via Stripe
  • To respond to support requests

Consent & email (CASL)

We only email you if you gave express consent (a checked box on our forms). Every email includes an unsubscribe option — reply STOP or use the link provided. We do not purchase, rent, or scrape contact lists. Any outreach beyond a direct reply requires internal human approval before it is sent.

How we protect it

API keys are stored hashed, never in plaintext. Secrets shown once are not re-displayed. Payment data never touches our servers — Stripe handles it under PCI-DSS. Website widget access uses a separate, limited public token; it cannot access your account settings or billing.

Who we share it with

Only the services required to run Meridian: Stripe (payment), Resend (transactional email), and your own downstream tools if you configure a webhook (your choice, your URL). We do not sell personal information.

Your rights

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, at any time. Contact us using the email your proposal was sent from, or reply STOP to any Meridian email to unsubscribe immediately.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the "Last updated" date above will change and, where required, we'll notify active customers by email.
