Optional legacy demos

OpenAI browser voice uses OPENAI_API_KEY on Railway. Legacy phone rollback also requires its own signed webhook and verified route. Optional ElevenLabs website speech uses its key and enable flag in Netlify Functions. None is required to configure the xAI phone path.

Connect an approved phone number.

  1. Register the number and signed webhookRegister the E.164 phone number with xAI. Use Meridian’s xAI webhook below and save the returned signing secret in Railway.
  2. Configure Twilio SIPPoint the trunk’s origination at the registered number’s xAI SIP address with TLS. Confirm media security and transfer support with your carrier.
  3. Verify the private route and billing gateMap the number to the correct approved agent. Check billing eligibility and the configured human/voicemail fallback.
  4. Record acceptance evidenceTest AI disclosure, natural audio, interruption, transfer, tool failures and actual calendar changes. Approve the rollback and client acceptance before activation.

This builder formats an address only. It does not buy, register, route or activate a phone number.
