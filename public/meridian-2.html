Questions

No. The model is to automate repetitive work and route exceptions to people.

Usually. Meridian is designed around integrations and the operating process.

Yes. They are capabilities inside the broader operating system.

Do you do autonomous cold outreach?

No. Outbound remains consent-aware and approval-controlled.

Managed operations can include monitoring, QA, reporting and optimization.
