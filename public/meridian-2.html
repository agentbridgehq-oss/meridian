Questions

Designed for operators, not hype.

Does Meridian replace my team?

No. The model is to automate repetitive work, improve response and route exceptions to people who should handle them.

Can you work with our existing tools?

Usually. Meridian is designed around integrations and the operating process, not forcing a business into one stack.

Do you offer voice, sales and booking agents?

Yes. They are capabilities inside the broader operating system rather than the whole Meridian proposition.

Do you do outbound prospecting automatically?

Outbound workflows remain consent-aware and approval-controlled. Meridian does not position autonomous cold blasting as a product feature.

What happens after launch?

Managed operations can include monitoring, QA, reporting, maintenance and workflow optimization.

How do we start?

Start with the opportunity scan. We identify the bottleneck, recommend the first system and define the next step.
